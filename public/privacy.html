Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

HomeSked ("we", "our", "us") operates the HomeSked mobile application and website at homesked.casa. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth). When you use the app, we store the home maintenance data you enter, including property details, systems, tasks, schedules, parts, documents, photos, and budget information. This data is stored in your account and synced to our cloud database so you can access it across devices.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and improve the HomeSked service, including: storing and syncing your maintenance data, sending optional email digests about upcoming and overdue tasks, processing payments through Stripe for paid tiers, and providing AI-powered features such as the maintenance advisor, manual extraction, and receipt scanning.

AI Features

When you use AI-powered features (maintenance advisor, manual upload extraction, receipt scanning), the content you submit is sent to Anthropic's API for processing. This data is used solely to generate your results and is not used to train AI models. Anthropic's usage policy applies to this processing.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services: Supabase (authentication and data storage), Stripe (payment processing), Vercel (website hosting), Anthropic (AI features), and Resend (email delivery). Each service has its own privacy policy governing the data they process on our behalf.

Payment Information

Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see, store, or have access to your credit card number or payment details. Stripe's privacy policy governs how your payment information is handled.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase with row-level security policies that ensure only you (and any service providers you authorize) can access your data. Data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Local Storage

The app stores some data locally on your device using browser storage to enable offline access and faster performance. This includes your maintenance data, preferences, and theme settings.

Photos and Documents

Photos and documents you upload are stored in Supabase Storage linked to your account. They are only accessible by you and are not shared with third parties except when you explicitly use AI features that process document content.

Amazon Associates

HomeSked participates in the Amazon Associates Program. When you click product links within the app, we may earn a small commission. Amazon's privacy policy governs your interaction with their services.

Children's Privacy

HomeSked is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You can export all your data at any time using the Export feature in the app. You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at support@homesked.casa. You can opt out of email digests at any time in the app settings.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where required by law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice in the app. Your continued use of HomeSked after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
